Coverage: NCAA Division I men's basketball from ESPN (via hoopR), seasons 2019-20 through 2025-26. The 2019-20 postseason was cancelled, so that season has no NCAA tournament games.

- season is the year the season ends: 2026 means 2025-26 and 2024 means 2023-24. seasons.label holds the "2025-26" form. Games in November and December belong to the season that ends the next year.
- date is TEXT 'YYYY-MM-DD'. Filter months with string ranges, e.g. date BETWEEN '2026-02-01' AND '2026-02-28'.
- Team names are ESPN display names that end in the mascot: 'Duke Blue Devils', 'UConn Huskies', "St. John's Red Storm", "Saint Mary's Gaels", 'Pennsylvania Quakers', 'Miami Hurricanes' (not 'Miami (OH) RedHawks'). Many schools share a first word ('Michigan Wolverines' and 'Michigan State Spartans'), so match the whole school name, e.g. name LIKE 'Michigan Wolverines%', never '%Michigan%'. team_id is ESPN's id (TEXT) and does not change across seasons.
- teams lists every team that appears in a game, including non-Division I opponents (conference NULL). teams.conference is only the most recent conference. Teams change conferences (Texas and Oklahoma moved from the Big 12 to the SEC for 2024-25; UCLA and USC from the Pac-12 to the Big Ten), so for a given season use team_seasons.conference.
- games has one row per completed game. margin = home_score - away_score: the home team won when margin > 0 and the away team when margin < 0. At neutral sites (neutral = 1) home and away are nominal. ot_periods counts overtimes. attendance is NULL when unknown.
- game_type is 'regular', 'conf_tournament', 'ncaa_tournament' (the NCAA tournament, First Four included) or 'other_postseason' (NIT, CBI and similar). season_type is ESPN's code: 2 = regular season including conference tournaments, 3 = postseason.
- tourney_round is set for NCAA tournament games only: 'First Four', '1st Round', '2nd Round', 'Sweet 16', 'Elite 8', 'Final Four', 'National Championship'.
- team_games has one row per team per game, from the box score, so each game appears twice. win is 1 or 0. poss is estimated possessions (fga - orb + tov + 0.475 * fta).
- team_seasons has one row per Division I team per season. games, wins and losses count every game that season, postseason and non-Division I opponents included. conf_wins and conf_losses count regular-season conference games only. ppg and opp_ppg are points scored and allowed per game.
- rating is a public power rating: a ridge regression on that season's regular-season games between Division I teams, in points better than an average Division I team on a neutral floor. rating_rank 1 is the best team. seasons.hca is that model's home-court advantage in points, and seasons.sigma is the typical miss in points.
- Rate stats in team_seasons are percentages on a 0-100 scale: efg_pct (effective field goal %), tov_pct (turnovers per 100 possessions), orb_pct (offensive rebound %), ft_rate (free throw attempts per 100 field goal attempts), fg3_rate (share of field goal attempts that are threes), fg3_pct. opp_efg_pct, opp_tov_pct and opp_ft_rate are the same for opponents, and drb_pct is defensive rebound %. tempo is possessions per 40 minutes. off_eff and def_eff are points scored and allowed per 100 possessions (a lower def_eff is a better defense), and net_eff = off_eff - def_eff.
- There is no player, coach, poll, betting, recruiting or arena data, and nothing before 2019-20 or after 2025-26.
