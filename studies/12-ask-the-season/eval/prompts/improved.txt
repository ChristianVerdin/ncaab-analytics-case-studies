Question: What was Baylor's conference record in 2022-23?

Question: What was Marquette's record in true road games (not neutral site) in 2021-22?
```sql
SELECT SUM(margin < 0) AS wins, SUM(margin > 0) AS losses
FROM games
WHERE season = 2022 AND neutral = 0
  AND away_id = (SELECT team_id FROM teams WHERE name LIKE 'Marquette Golden Eagles%')
```

Question: How many Pac-12 teams played in the 2023 NCAA tournament?
```sql
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT ts.team_id)
FROM games g
JOIN team_seasons ts ON ts.season = g.season AND ts.team_id IN (g.home_id, g.away_id)
WHERE g.season = 2023 AND g.game_type = 'ncaa_tournament' AND ts.conference = 'Pac-12'
```
